Original C12 fish, rocket and house coordinate designs; no external artwork or competitor inputs. Each was designed for 26 points, not reduced from a longer worksheet. Source: scripts/alphabet/designs.json; task-local adapter: scripts/alphabet/build.py reuses original-sheets geometry/collision helpers. geometry.json contains points, per-case label boxes, solid details, font and parameters. Standard PDF Times-Roman 14 pt provides a serif lowercase l; no font binaries. Same closed geometry on both papers; A4 vertically centered, Letter horizontally centered. PNGs rendered from each corresponding final PDF. English alphabet only. No age, learning-effect, solve-time or Large Print validation. Physical print and independent full human solve NOT TESTED.
