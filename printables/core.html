How to Get the Optimal Experience: Printing and Usage Guide

Easy Steps for a Perfect Print

  1. Scaling Check: Before printing, always confirm the setting is on “100% Scale” to ensure the dots remain in their exact intended positions without proportional error.
  2. Preventing Smudging: Use a standard, uncoated white paper. If using markers to color the final image, insert a piece of scrap paper underneath to prevent bleed onto the next sheet.
  3. Tool Consistency: Use a consistent writing instrument throughout the puzzle. Switching from a pencil to a thick pen can change the perceived accuracy of the connections.
